# Dashboard assets

The dashboard serves its fonts, icons and image locally. No external image or font request is needed to render it.

- `dashboard/public/research-desk.png`: generated editorial illustration, 1536 × 1024 pixels. Created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-06. It represents research materials, not a real newsroom or evidence for a claim. Original generated output is preserved; the repository contains a copy.
- `dashboard/public/mark.svg`: project-created vector mark. It identifies this independent project and does not imply endorsement by X.
- Manrope and Newsreader: bundled through Fontsource. Their Open Font Licenses are reproduced in `dashboard/public/font-licenses.txt`.
- Interface icons: Lucide, supplied by the `lucide-react` dependency under its license.

## Image prompt

> Use case: editorial photographic illustration. Asset: one wide landscape image for a professional local X/Twitter research dashboard, not a screenshot. Quiet researcher's desk photographed from above at a slight angle: beautifully folded monochrome newspapers with columns of intentionally unreadable small print, a black pencil, and a slender steel magnifying glass over paper. Off-white paper, charcoal ink, muted warm stone tabletop. Natural window light from the upper left, subtle grain, tactile textures, refined understated magazine art direction. Objects composed toward the center and right with generous breathing room. No people, no logos, no social-media icons, no charts, no readable headlines, no watermarks. Wide 3:2 composition. It should look like a real editorial still life, with crisp details and calm contrast; no neon, futuristic glow, or digital interface overlays.
